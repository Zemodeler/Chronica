# NPC social dialogue and commitments

NPC dialogue is concise, relationship-aware, and grounded in each character's means, pressures, memories, and current sentiment. A durable declared connection (for example, sibling or family friend) is distinct from mutable sentiment.

Chat records a credible promised commitment alongside the NPC reply, keyed to that reply so retries cannot duplicate it. Chat never applies money, goods, authority, or other material effects directly. Turn resolution is the sole authority: it validates availability, conditions, reachability, and bounded value; then fulfills, partially fulfills, defers, fails, or cancels it with an in-world reason and Chronicle provenance.
