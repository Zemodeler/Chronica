# NPC social dialogue and commitments

NPC dialogue is concise, relationship-aware, and grounded in each character's means, pressures, memories, and current sentiment. A durable declared connection (for example, sibling or family friend) is distinct from mutable sentiment.

Chat may record a credible promised commitment, but it must never apply money, goods, authority, or other material effects directly. A follow-up implementation will store idempotent pending commitments and let turn resolution alone validate and apply bounded outcomes, then record provenance in the Chronicle and NPC memory.
