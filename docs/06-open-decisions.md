# Open decisions

These are questions to resolve with the project owner. They are not commitments.

## Scenario and world

- What is the final geographical and political scope of the first playable First Punic War scenario?
- What fidelity and delivery workflow should follow the first author-supplied PNG and GeoJSON pair?
- Which other historical periods or curated scenarios should follow the demo?

## AI and operations

- What model, budget per turn, latency target, and failure/retry behavior are acceptable for the OpenAI integration?
- Should historical lookup remain model/scenario knowledge only, or later use approved external sources? If so, which sources, citations, and cache policy are acceptable?
- What safety, privacy, and retention policy applies to player-authored character descriptions and conversation history?

## Simulation and experience

- What precise combat inputs and outcomes belong in the first rules-backed resolver?
- How large should a scenario be in realms, provinces, settlements, and active forces?
- Which information belongs in each left-edge panel, and which must remain visible on the map?
- Should the latest-turn autosave later expand to named saves, rewind, or replay controls?

## Future phases

- What relationship and ambition systems should lead Phase 2?
- Which schemes/secrets and diplomacy/politics features are worth their complexity?
- After Phase 2, should Phase 3 first deepen simulation, add scenarios/eras, or introduce creator tools?
