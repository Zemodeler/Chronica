# North Africa in 270 BCE: who held what

Audit of the ownership in `packages/db/src/punic-wars-map-graph.ts` (generated by
`scripts/map-gen/build-map-graph.ts`) west of the Nile, against the record for 270 BCE. Corrections are
data, in `scripts/map-gen/ownership-corrections-northafrica.json`: each assignment is a polygon in
WGS84, a province belongs to it when its centre lies inside, later assignments win, and nothing is
applied yet. Egypt's own evidence is in `docs/egypt-arabia-270-bce.md`.

How the current map got its owners: the old 780-province map was overlaid by area, smoothed, and then
open desert (no town, no river or coast corridor, 80 km from every town) was cut out and left to no
one. The province set here is only a coastal and pre-Saharan strip, so the Sahara proper is off the
map except one province at Garama. The scenario polities are in `packages/db/src/punic-wars-scenario.ts`.

## Judgement calls

- **Cyrene is Magas, not a Ptolemaic province.** Ptolemy I sent Magas about 300 to reconquer the
  region; after Ptolemy I died in 283 he made himself king (put at 276), married Apama of Antiochus I,
  marched on Egypt while the Seleucid attacked, and was called home by a revolt of the Libyan
  Marmaridae. He ruled until 250 and reconciled with Egypt by betrothing Berenice II to Ptolemy III.
  So the id `cyrene` is an independent monarchy in 270, hostile to Egypt, with the Catabathmus as the
  frontier. The scenario already gives it a monarchy government.
- **Carthage's eastern frontier is the Altars of the Philaeni**, at the south of the Gulf of Sidra
  near Ras Lanuf, which became the boundary of Tripolitania and Cyrenaica (Sallust, Jugurtha 79). The
  Syrtic coast between Leptis and the altars is thin and nominal, so this is medium confidence.
- **The Egyptian west edge** is the Catabathmus (Sollum, 25.15E). Paraetonium and the Marmarica coast
  are Ptolemaic stations. The oases up to the Nile are counted Egyptian as in `docs/egypt-arabia-270-bce.md`.
- **Mauri and Masaesyli meet at the Muluya** (Strabo 17.3.9; Masaesyli "from the Mulucha"). Only the
  coast and the lower river are firm; the line inland is a straight sketch.
- **The Gaetuli belt.** Gaetuli are nomads from the Atlantic to the Syrtis with no state in 270. The
  belt is a consolidation of the scattered patches and it cuts against the rule that open desert
  is held by no one; the deep Sahara south of about 30N stays unowned. It is the lead's call.
- **Punic posts** on the Atlantic (Lixus, Mogador, Kerne) and on the Algerian coast are not drawn.
  The Hanno colonies had mostly lapsed, and Tingis is fixed Mauretanian by the scenario.

## Should `numidian-kingdoms` split into Massyli and Masaesyli?

Recommendation: no, not for a 270 start. The two kingdoms are named only in the Second Punic War
(Livy, Polybius: Syphax of the Masaesyli, Gala and Massinissa of the Massyli, from about 213). For 270
there is no dateable king of either, and the map already holds `massyli` and `massaesyli` as regions,
so the split can be drawn later at about 5.0E without moving the map. The game consequence of a
split is a second monarchy to seed (character, government, standard, diplomacy), a second neighbour
for Carthage to fight in the Mercenary War of 241-238, and no gain before that. Keep one polity, and
reopen it if a scenario ever starts after 240.
