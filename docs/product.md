# Chronica product vision

Chronica is a map-first, single-player historical strategy game played through one character in a persistent political world. You investigate, speak, make promises, and give orders in ordinary language; the world answers through the people, institutions, resources, and consequences that already exist within it.

The ambition is not a historical chatbot or a province-management spreadsheet. It is a world where an order has weight: a commander can refuse it, a journey takes time, an office grants and limits authority, money and forces are finite, and an unfinished decision remains part of the world until something actually changes it.

## The player experience

Each campaign begins from a curated, validated historical or fictional scenario. Its map, places, forces, offices, resources, conflicts, and people are authored facts. The player inhabits one character and uses conversation and orders to pursue their aims.

The map gives events a place and makes movement, control, distance, and war legible. It is a view into the world rather than an administrative command board. Character panels, conversations, world status, and the Chronicle make the same persistent world understandable from different angles.

The player is not limited to a fixed verb menu. They can ask for a surrender, order a march only if negotiations fail, offer money within a limit, or pursue a political arrangement. The request is an intent, not a magic command: it succeeds only when a lawful, validated action can carry it out.

## A living world

Characters have canonical identities and persistent circumstances. Their public situation, relationships, beliefs, pressures, commitments, and memories are distinct state, not disposable dialogue. They can pursue their own interests, react to facts they plausibly know, refuse requests, die, and be succeeded.

The world is consequential because its material state is real. Authority belongs to offices and institutions; forces, accounts, territory, and commitments have owners and limits. AI supplies interpretation and character judgment where judgment is appropriate. The engine protects identities, authority, resources, timing, and replay safety.

The Chronicle is the readable history of what happened. It is downstream of facts and cannot invent causes or outcomes. An accepted action, a real refusal, and an unresolved technical attempt are deliberately different things.

## Product boundaries

Version 1.0 focuses on:

- single-player, curated campaigns;
- a persistent player character and a living political world;
- map, conversation, orders, status views, and Chronicle;
- rules-backed military, political, social, and material consequences.

It does not promise multiplayer, free-form AI world generation, tactical control, a map-management interface, or a separate planning dashboard.
